# Crucible installer (two paths)

Scope: the replacement for tinker.wc8mi.net/crucible/: a browser gate, terms, a two-door home, the Install work card
and the scan-first Update path. Prototype and screens in docs/crucible/adr/001/ (ADR 001). Mode: Operate. World: the
established foundry world in DESIGN.md (inherited, not changed).
Audience: appliance operators, low technical skill, Chrome or Edge on a computer. Job: install Crucible on a radio with
the manufacturer's firmware, with a backup first; or update a radio on Crucible or RadioFoundry. Constraints: ASD-STE100
words; no RadioFoundry services; the shared flasher engine stays upstream's.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole job on one work card: every step visible from the start, one open at a time, done steps closed to
their result. Refuses the wizard that hides how many screens remain, and upstream's six-task menu.

OWN-WORLD: The foundry world unchanged: hot-core band with the single-cut mark, iron-black or greensand ground, iron
plates with square shoulders, Barlow Condensed uppercase casting-mark step names and numerals, hot-core numerals on the
open step, a hot-core check on done ones; line-drawn radios; every progress bar a pour, the step bar included.

STORY: The visitor learns the browser works, accepts the risk once, picks Install or Update, sees the whole procedure,
saves their channels before anything is written, puts the radio in update mode with a drawn key diagram, watches the
pour, and checks the result. Advanced users connect, open Other versions, pick, install.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the question as a large casting-mark heading; two equal door plates side by side, each a
line-drawn radio, a two-word title, one sentence and a verb button, both buttons hot-core for equal weight (Install's mark is the only lit art); small links below;
the four radios listed under it.

FORM: own grounded list, candidate 3 of 7 (the work card); seed:67fd2aeb5bd68219. Signature interaction: a step
closes to one line with its result as the next one opens, and the pour bar above the list advances with it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
